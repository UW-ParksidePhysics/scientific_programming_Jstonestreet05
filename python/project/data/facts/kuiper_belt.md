# Kuiper Belt

## Overview
A human-centered description of the Kuiper Belt as a reservoir of icy, primitive bodies beyond Neptune.

## Exploration history
- Survey programs and targeted missions have begun to reveal the diversity of Kuiper Belt objects.
- New Horizons’ exploration of a Kuiper Belt object provided a close-up view of a representative member.

## Scientific highlights
- Plain-language explanation of why Kuiper Belt objects preserve early Solar System materials and what they reveal about formation processes.
- The belt’s role as a source of short-period comets.

## Cultural and media examples
- Works that explore trans‑Neptunian space or use Kuiper Belt objects as evocative settings.
