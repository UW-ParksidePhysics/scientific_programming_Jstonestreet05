# Venus

## Overview
A qualitative description of Venus as a world with a dense, cloud‑shrouded atmosphere and extreme greenhouse conditions that make its surface hostile.

## Exploration history
- Radar mapping and atmospheric probe missions revealed the planet’s surface and the composition of its clouds.
- Longstanding interest in Venusian geology and atmospheric chemistry has driven multiple mission concepts.

## Scientific highlights
- Conceptual explanation of the runaway greenhouse effect and why Venus is a cautionary example for climate science.
- Discussion of atmospheric dynamics and cloud chemistry in plain language.

## Cultural and media examples
- Works of fiction and art that imagine Venusian landscapes or use Venus as a metaphor for extreme environments.
