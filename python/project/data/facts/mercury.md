# Mercury

## Overview
A concise, qualitative portrait of Mercury as a small, heavily cratered world with extreme surface conditions and a surprisingly large metallic core.

## Exploration history
- Early telescopic observations led to hypotheses about surface markings and polar caps.
- Flybys and orbiters provided high-resolution imagery and revealed Mercury’s magnetic field and tectonic features.
