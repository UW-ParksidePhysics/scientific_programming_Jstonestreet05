# Voyager 2

## Overview
Voyager 2 is a NASA space probe that performed a grand tour of the outer planets and continues to send data from the outer heliosphere.

## Mission narrative
- Voyager 2’s unique trajectory enabled flybys of Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune, providing first close-up observations of the ice giants.
- After planetary encounters, the probe entered an extended mission to study the heliosphere and interstellar medium.

## Scientific highlights
- Voyager 2 provided the only in-situ measurements of Uranus and Neptune and their moons, greatly expanding knowledge of the outer Solar System.
- Its instruments continue to provide complementary data to Voyager 1 for heliospheric science.

## Cultural and media examples
- The mission’s achievements are featured in documentaries and books that celebrate human exploration.
- Voyager 2’s story is often cited in discussions of long-duration robotic missions and engineering longevity.
