# Mars

## Overview
Mars is a small, rocky world that has long captured scientific and public imagination as the most Earthlike planet in the inner Solar System.

## Exploration history
- Early telescopic observations suggested polar caps and surface markings.
- Robotic exploration progressed from flybys and orbiters to landers and rovers that mapped geology and searched for past water.
- Recent missions focus on sample caching and preparing for future sample return and human exploration.

## Scientific highlights
- Mars preserves a record of early planetary processes and climate change that helps us understand habitability.
- Polar layered deposits and sedimentary rocks record past environmental conditions.

## Cultural and media examples
- **The Mars Trilogy** by Kim Stanley Robinson — longform exploration of colonization and terraforming themes.
- **The Martian** by Andy Weir — survival and engineering on Mars presented in accessible, realistic detail.
- Films, games, and art often use Mars as a setting for human expansion and frontier narratives.
