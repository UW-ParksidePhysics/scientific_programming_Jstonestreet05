# Jupiter

## Overview
Jupiter is the dominant giant of the Solar System, a dynamic world whose gravity and magnetosphere shape the environment of the outer planets and small bodies.

## Exploration history
- Early telescopic study revealed banded clouds and long-lived storms.
- Spacecraft flybys and orbiters provided detailed views of atmospheric dynamics, magnetospheric structure, and the Galilean moons.
- Recent missions emphasize deep atmosphere studies and moon habitability.

## Scientific highlights
- Jupiter acts as a laboratory for fluid dynamics and magnetohydrodynamics at planetary scale.
- Its moon system includes worlds with subsurface oceans that are prime targets in the search for life.

## Cultural and media examples
- Many science fiction works use Jupiter and its moons as settings for exploration, colonization, or exotic environments.
- Notable themes include the contrast between the hostile giant and potentially habitable icy moons.
