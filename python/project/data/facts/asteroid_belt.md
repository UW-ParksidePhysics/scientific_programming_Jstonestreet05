# Asteroid Belt

## Overview
A qualitative description of the Asteroid Belt as a diverse population of small bodies between the inner and outer planets.

## Exploration history
- Missions and surveys have characterized a range of objects from dust to dwarf planets and revealed compositional diversity.
- Sample studies and meteorite research link belt objects to material delivered to Earth.

## Scientific highlights
- Plain-language notes on how asteroid populations inform models of Solar System formation and collisional evolution.
- The role of the belt in delivering volatiles and organics to the inner Solar System.
