# Voyager 1

## Overview
Voyager 1 is a landmark robotic mission that performed flybys of the outer planets and continues to return unique measurements from the outer heliosphere and interstellar space.

## Mission narrative
- Launched as part of a grand tour, Voyager 1 provided transformative imagery and data for Jupiter and Saturn.
- After its planetary encounters, the probe entered an extended mission to study the heliosphere and interstellar medium.
- Voyager 1 carries the Golden Record, a cultural artifact intended to represent Earth to any future discoverers.

## Scientific highlights
- Voyager 1’s instruments measured plasma, magnetic fields, and cosmic rays, enabling the first direct study of the heliopause.
- The mission’s long baseline of observations remains invaluable for understanding solar and interstellar environments.

## Cultural and media examples
- The Golden Record has inspired documentaries, essays, and artistic projects about humanity’s place in the cosmos.
- Voyager’s story appears in numerous books and films about exploration and the deep future.
