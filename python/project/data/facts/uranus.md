# Uranus

## Overview
A concise, qualitative portrait of Uranus as an ice giant with an extreme axial tilt and subtle ring and moon systems.

## Exploration history
- Voyager 2’s flyby provided the first close-up observations and remains the primary in-situ dataset.
- Subsequent telescopic and remote observations have refined knowledge of composition and seasonal behavior.
